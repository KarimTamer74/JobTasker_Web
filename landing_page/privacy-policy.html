Privacy Policy

At JobTasker, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We prioritize collecting minimal data necessary for the app's functionality:

  • Task Data: Your tasks, categories, and focus sessions are stored locally on your device.
  • Job Applications: Details about your job applications are stored locally.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve app performance (e.g., crash logs).

2. Local Storage

JobTasker is designed as a "Local First" application. This means effectively all your personal data (tasks, job entries) remains on your device and is not synced to our servers unless you explicitly use a cloud backup feature (if available in future versions).

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect data to provide personalized ads. You can manage your ad preferences through your device settings.

4. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally, it is protected by your device's security measures (passcode, biometrics). We recommend keeping your device secure.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
